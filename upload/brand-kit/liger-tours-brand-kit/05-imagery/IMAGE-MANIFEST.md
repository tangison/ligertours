## Missing images (to pull)
Pull properly licensed photos into `web/` and log every one in `image-license-log.csv`. Needed:
1. Elephants or wildlife at a waterhole (Etosha-type scene), sharp, 2400px wide minimum
2. Swakopmund coast: dunes meeting the Atlantic, Walvis Bay flamingos
3. Fish River Canyon, Spitzkoppe, Damaraland rock arches
4. People: guests and guide in the field, candid, faces visible, with consent. Best source is the client's own photos
5. Himba/Herero or Ovambo cultural experiences ONLY from sources with clear consent and licence
6. Campfire / camp at dusk, sundowner scene
7. Night sky over dunes

Sources allowed: client's own photos first, then Unsplash, Pexels, Pixabay, Wikimedia Commons (check each image's licence; CC-BY needs attribution shown). Free to use does not mean copyright-free: record licence and author for every image. Never take images from other tour operator websites, Google Images or Pinterest.
Never generate images with AI.
